**Full description** (4000 max)

```
Fortun0 shows you the one number most money apps don't: what is actually safe to spend. Not your bank balance, but what is left after every EMI, bill and card payment due before your next payday.

Built for India: rupees in lakhs and crores, loans the way Indian banks calculate them, and reminders that work even without internet.

KNOW WHERE YOU STAND
• Safe to spend until payday, and what that means per day
• Expected balance at month end, based on your dues and how you have been spending
• This month's income, spending and what remains, at a glance
• Charts for daily spending, categories and the last six months

EVERY EMI, TO THE LAST RUPEE
• Home, personal, bike, car, education, gold, phone and credit card EMIs
• The full repayment schedule for each loan: principal, interest and balance, month by month
• Paid something extra? Record it against the principal and see how many EMIs and how much interest it saves
• See how much of your total borrowing you have paid off

NEVER MISS A DUE DATE
• Reminders before EMIs, bills and credit card payments are due, on the day, and if they become overdue
• Recurring bills: rent, electricity, recharges, insurance premiums, SIPs
• Mark as paid in one tap; your balance updates itself

ALL YOUR MONEY IN ONE PLACE
• Cash in hand, bank accounts, wallets, deposits and investments
• Note what each account is for: salary, daily spends, emergency fund
• Credit cards with limit, amount used, available limit and due date
• Record expenses against the right bank account, cash or card

YOUR FINANCIAL HEALTH
• A score out of 100 from five things that matter: EMI burden, credit card usage, emergency buffer, savings margin and insurance cover
• A plain-language tip for each one
• A money profile with your own avatar and a money personality that fits your situation

STAY SAFE FROM SCAMS
• A clear guide to the frauds people in India face most: OTP calls, UPI collect and QR tricks, "digital arrest" calls, screen-sharing apps, fake KYC messages and loan apps
• One tap to call the national cyber crime helpline, 1930

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Fortun0 never reads your SMS and never asks for bank logins. It only knows what you enter.
• Lock the app with your fingerprint, face or phone PIN
• Use it with no account at all, with everything kept on your phone
• Or create a free account to back up your data and restore it on a new phone
• No ads. Your data is never sold.
• Delete your account and all its data at any time from Settings

Fortun0 is a tracking and planning tool. It does not lend money, hold your money or sell financial products. Scores and tips are general guidance based on what you enter, not financial advice.
```

## App content declarations

Answer these as the app stands today. Revisit them when premium, ads or AI features are added.

**Privacy policy:** https://app-paisapilot.web.app/privacy.html

**Ads:** No, the app does not contain ads.

**App access:** Some features need sign-in, so give the reviewer a way in. Simplest instruction to enter:
"Tap Skip on the introduction, then 'Continue without an account'. Every feature is available this way; an account only adds cloud backup."

**Content rating questionnaire:** Category "Utility, Productivity, Communication or Other". No violence, sexual content, profanity, drugs or gambling. Users cannot interact or share content with each other. The app does not share the user's location. Expected result: rated for everyone.

**Target audience:** 18 and over only. The app is not designed for children.

**News app:** No. **Health app:** No. **Government app:** No.

**Financial features:** The app does not provide loans, banking, payments, investing, insurance sales or crypto. If the form offers "My app doesn't provide any financial features", that is the accurate choice for a tracker. If Google's wording at the time makes "financial advice" or "money management" look like a closer fit, stop and check before submitting, because some options require an Organisation account.
