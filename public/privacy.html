Fortun0 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Fortun0 is an app for tracking your EMIs, bills and money. It is developed and operated by Akhil Padmanaban ("we"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • The app only knows what you type into it. It does not read your SMS, connect to your bank, or access your contacts.
  • You can use it with no account, in which case your data never leaves your phone.
  • If you create an account, your data is backed up to Google Firebase so you can restore it on another phone.
  • We do not sell your data and the app shows no ads.
  • You can delete your account and all of its data at any time.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Email address and nameIf you create an accountTo sign you in, verify it is you and reset your password
Profile details you enter: avatar choice, age, monthly income, payday, other income, estimated EMIs, how you rate your confidence about moneyIf you fill them inTo calculate figures such as safe-to-spend and your financial health score, and to personalise guidance
Financial records you enter: accounts and balances, credit card limits and dues, loans, bills, transactionsAs you use the appTo provide the app's features
A push notification token for your deviceWhen the app startsTo be able to send you notifications
Crash reports: device model, Android version, app version, a random installation ID and the technical details of the crashIf the app crashesTo find and fix bugs

We never ask for, and you should never enter, full card numbers, CVVs, PINs, net-banking passwords or OTPs. The app only asks for the last four digits of an account or card, and only so you can tell them apart.

Where your data is kept

Without an account: everything stays in the app's private storage on your phone. We cannot see it. Uninstalling the app or choosing "Erase all data" in Settings removes it.

With an account: a copy is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, hosted in Mumbai, India, and Firebase Authentication). It is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google. Access rules allow each signed-in, email-verified user to read and write only their own data.

Who we share it with

Nobody, other than Google as our service provider for the following:

  • Firebase Authentication, to manage sign-in
  • Cloud Firestore, to store your backup
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging, to deliver notifications
  • Firebase Crashlytics, to collect crash reports

Google processes this data on our behalf under its own terms: firebase.google.com/support/privacy. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Your account data is kept until you delete your account. Crash reports are kept by Firebase for 90 days.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your data: everything we hold is visible and editable in the app.
  • Delete your account: in the app, go to More > Settings > Delete account. This permanently removes your data from our servers and your phone. You can also request deletion without the app: see how to delete your account.
  • Use the app without an account: choose "Continue without an account".
  • Turn off notifications: in More > Settings, or in your phone's settings.

If you are in India, you have rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including to access, correct and erase your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to raise a grievance with us using the contact below.

Security

You can lock the app with your fingerprint, face or phone PIN. The app is excluded from Android's cloud and device-to-device backups. No system can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Not financial advice

Figures, scores and tips in the app are based only on what you enter and are for general guidance. They are not financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date above. Significant changes will also be notified in the app.
