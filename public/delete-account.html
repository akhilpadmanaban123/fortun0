Delete your Fortun0 account

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Fortun0 and sign in.
  2. Go to More > Settings.
  3. Under "Backup & account", tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your password to confirm.

Your account and data are deleted straight away.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email codeitup1234@gmail.com from the email address your account uses, with the subject "Delete my Fortun0 account". We will delete the account within 7 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (email address and password)
  • Your profile: name, avatar, age, income details and confidence rating
  • All accounts, credit cards, loans, extra payments, bills and transactions you entered
  • Your device's notification token

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you. Anonymous crash reports already sent are removed automatically after 90 days.

If you used the app without an account, there is nothing on our servers. Uninstalling the app, or choosing "Erase all data" in Settings, removes everything from your phone.
